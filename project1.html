Adaptive Knee Brace

← Back to Home

Overview

This project explores the design of a knee brace that increases stiffness under load using viscoelastic materials, providing both flexibility and support.

Problem

Traditional knee braces are either rigid or flexible, limiting their effectiveness across different activity levels.

Design Approach

The brace uses strategically placed viscoelastic elements that respond to force, increasing resistance during high-load conditions.

Build & Fabrication

  • 3D printed structural components
  • Mechanical joint design
  • Material testing for viscoelastic response

Results

Initial prototypes demonstrated increased stiffness under load while maintaining flexibility during normal motion.

Skills Demonstrated

  • CAD Design
  • Mechanical Prototyping
  • Material Selection
