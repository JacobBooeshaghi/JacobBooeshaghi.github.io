About

About Me

A short introduction to my background, interests, and current work.

Background

I am a biomedical engineering student at the University of Florida's Honors Program. My work centers on combining research, CAD, prototyping, and fabrication to build useful physical systems.

Interests

I am especially interested in tissue engineering, microfluidics, assistive devices, and hands-on manufacturing. Outside of biomedical work, I spend a lot of time blacksmithing and developing practical fabrication skills.

Current Work

I am currently working on perfusable tissue constructs, a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip system for Type 1 diabetes research, and real-time control systems for an assistive exoskeleton.
