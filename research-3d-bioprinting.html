Research

3D Bioprinting

Evaluating print fidelity of gelatin and gelatin-microgel bioinks with quantitative ImageJ analysis.

Project Goal

This project tested print fidelity across multiple gelatin and gelatin-microgel bioink concentrations to identify the best-performing formulation. Fidelity was assessed by comparing expected vs actual cross-sectional area using quantitative image analysis in ImageJ.
